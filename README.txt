- quote.html — Quote request form with an illustrative planning estimate

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Contact details and hours are now filled in on contact.html. Double-check Wednesday hours (listed as 9 AM-5 PM).
2. Quote requests are sent directly to ezpackshipmore@gmail.com (set in script.js, line with 'const recipient').
3. The quote estimate is weight x $14 per lb (change RATE in script.js). It excludes customs; documents get no price. Customs clearance is offered for Nepal only.
4. FormSubmit email delivery requires email verification/activation on first use. Test it with a real submission before launch. The very first submission sends an activation email to that address; click the confirm link in it, otherwise requests will not be delivered. Requests only send from the live website, not from a file opened on your computer.
5. Review the service descriptions in services.html (Graphic Design replaced Packing Services).
6. A Google Maps embed for 4157 W Northgate Dr, Irving, TX is on contact.html.

HOW TO PREVIEW
Open index.html in a web browser. For best results, upload the entire folder to a static host such as Netlify, Vercel, or your web host.

DOMAIN / HOSTING
You have not purchased a domain or hosting yet. The site files are ready for preview and can be deployed after choosing a host and domain. No domain has been purchased or connected by this package.

HOME PAGE PHOTO SLIDER
Put store photos in the images folder named slide-01.jpg to slide-12.jpg (landscape, about 1200px wide works best).
Only photos that exist are shown; missing numbers are skipped automatically, so there is nothing to delete.
If no photos exist at all, a branded logo slide is shown instead.

ICONS
Icons are drawn by script.js (the ICONS list at the top) and used in pages as <svg class="icon"><use href="#i-NAME"/></svg>.
